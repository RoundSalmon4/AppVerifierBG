# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a small project maintained in my spare time. Be respectful and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or hostile language
- Spam, off-topic, or deliberately disruptive issues and comments
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

I reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, and block contributors without warning
at my sole discretion. This is a personal project, not a public forum — I will not tolerate
abuse directed at me or other community members.
